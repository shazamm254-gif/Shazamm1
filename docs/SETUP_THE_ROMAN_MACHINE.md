# Setting up The Roman Machine

Everything here is doable from the phone. Assets are in
[`brand/roman-machine/`](../brand/roman-machine/).

## 1. Make the channel — not a new Google account

YouTube app → your avatar → **Switch account** → **Add channel**. This creates
a second channel under the login you already have, so both sit in one
switcher and you never log out to post.

A separate Google account buys nothing and costs friction on every upload.

## 2. Handle

Try `@TheRomanMachine`. The Roman Shorts space is crowded, so have fallbacks
ready before you announce anything: `@RomanMachineHQ`, `@TheRomanMachineYT`.
Pick the handle before the art — it is the one thing you cannot change freely
later.

## 3. Art

| File | Where |
|---|---|
| `avatar_800.png` | Profile picture |
| `banner_2048.png` | Banner |

The avatar is drawn for the circular crop and checked at 24px, which is the
size it appears at beside a comment — that is where most channel art falls
apart. The banner keeps the whole wordmark inside the 1235×338 area that
survives on every device; `banner_safearea_check.png` shows what gets cropped.

Type is Anton, the same face burned into the captions, so the page and the
videos read as one thing.

## 4. Description

```
How the Roman Empire actually worked — the machinery of power, spectacle and punishment, and the people it ground through.

Not a facts channel. Every video takes one thing Rome did and shows the system that produced it, with the source on screen.

New Shorts weekly.
```

## 5. Channel keywords

```
roman history, ancient rome, roman empire, ancient history, history shorts, roman army, colosseum, roman britain, roman legion, history facts
```

## 6. First three uploads

Both finished videos are ready with metadata written.

1. **A Million Nails** — [`METADATA_ROME_BEASTS.md`](METADATA_ROME_BEASTS.md) has the
   damnatio metadata; the nails title and description are in the launch pack.
   Post this first: strongest hook, least like anything else in the niche.
2. **The Fire Brigade** — the villain hook, and it names its source on screen.
3. **Damnatio ad bestias** — [`METADATA_ROME_BEASTS.md`](METADATA_ROME_BEASTS.md).

Leave a day or two between them. Three videos is enough for the format to be
visible; posting them in one hour is not.

## 7. What not to bother with yet

No trailer, no playlists, no community posts, no schedule graphic. None of it
moves a Shorts channel at zero subscribers, and all of it is time not spent on
video four.

The only thing that compounds early is **posting**.
