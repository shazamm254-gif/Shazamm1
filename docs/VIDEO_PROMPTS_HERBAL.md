# Herbs Decoded — Paste-Ready Motion Prompts (4 shots × 10 Shorts)

Written for **Veo 3** (works in other video models too, minus the audio
lines). Each prompt describes camera movement, subject motion, lighting, and
a native audio cue — Veo 3 generates sound, so let the sizzle and steam do
work for you. Generate at **vertical 9:16**, ~8 seconds per clip; you'll trim
to 1.5–3 seconds per beat in the edit, so favor slow, continuous motion that
survives being cut anywhere.

**The channel look (baked into every prompt):** vivid botanical color glowing
against a near-black background, one soft directional light, macro detail,
slow deliberate camera moves. Matches the stills in
`IMAGE_PROMPTS_HERBAL.md` — same numbering, same beats.

Rules of thumb for this niche:
- One motion per clip. A push-in OR a rotation, not both.
- Keep voiceover as your only speech — prompt for ambient sound, never dialogue.
- Add "no text, no captions, no watermark" if a model starts adding overlays.

## Veo 3 workflow tips

- **8s generations, 2–3s cuts.** Generate the full clip, then cut the best
  1.5–3 seconds in the edit — slow continuous motion gives you freedom to cut
  anywhere.
- **Mix the native audio low.** Veo's sizzles, cracks, and steam are texture
  under your voiceover — duck them to roughly -20dB under the VO, and mute
  any clip where the model added unwanted sound.
- **No people talking.** If a human appears, add "no dialogue, no lip
  movement" — the voiceover is the only voice on the channel.
- **First frame = thumbnail.** For each Short's opening clip, pick the
  generation whose *first frame* already works as a thumbnail (herb large,
  top third clear for text) — see `THUMBNAIL_CHECKLIST_HERBAL.md`.
- **Image-to-video option:** if a still from `IMAGE_PROMPTS_HERBAL.md` came
  out perfect, feed it to Veo as the starting frame and keep only the motion
  and audio lines from the matching prompt here.
