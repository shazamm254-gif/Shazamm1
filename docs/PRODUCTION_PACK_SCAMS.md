# The Setup — All-in-One Production Pack (phone-friendly)

Everything needed to make all 10 Shorts, in one file. No jumping between
documents, no wide tables, nothing to cross-reference. Built to be read and
worked from on a phone.

Every image prompt below is **complete and self-contained** — the style suffix
is already baked into each one. Copy the whole block, paste it into your image
tool, set vertical 9:16, generate. Your shots will match across all ten videos
without you tracking anything.

## How to make one Short (the loop)

1. Pick a Short below.
2. **Generate the shots** — copy each prompt block into your AI image or video
   tool (Midjourney, Flux, Leonardo, Runway, Kling…), 3–4 variations each, keep
   the coldest one. The style is written into the prompt, so they'll match.
3. **Add the text yourself.** Never generate the words — image models mangle
   small type. The prompts deliberately produce *blank* screens, bubbles and
   documents. Set the actual text in your editor. This is the biggest
   time-saver in the whole system.
4. **Record the voiceover** — calm, low, forensic. Never entertained. Roughly
   2.5 words per second; every VO here runs 43–47 seconds.
5. **Edit** — lay shots over the voice using the timings given, one quiet
   music bed, on-screen captions.
6. **Post** — use the TITLE and DESCRIPTION provided, add the standing
   boilerplate below, and reply to early comments.

Batch all 10 in one sitting, then schedule one a day.

## The camera is three moves

Slow push-in on a reveal. Slow pull-back on a scale reveal. Hard cut on the
twist. Nothing else — no whips, no shakes, no zooms. Anything busier makes this
material look like the content it's critiquing.

## The rules that keep this monetized

- Explain **how victims are manipulated**, never how to run the play.
- No real brands, logos or institution names — invent them (`NORTHBANK`,
  `MERIDIAN TRUST`). A real bank's mark on a fraud video is a trademark and a
  defamation problem at once.
- Never produce a usable fake — no working QR code, no convincing replica of a
  real login page or invoice.
- No real person's face, living or historical.
- Never mock the victim. The thesis is always "this was built to work on you."

## Description boilerplate (append to every video)

> Educational only. This channel explains how scams work so they stop working.
> If you think you've been targeted, report it to your local fraud authority
> and contact your bank directly — using the number on your own card.

## 9 — "The call that warns you" · *Bank impersonation*

**HOOK (0–2s):** "The best scam call doesn't ask for your money. It warns you someone else is taking it."

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** "YOUR ACCOUNT IS AT RISK"

**VOICEOVER:**
> The most effective scam call doesn't ask you for money. It warns you that someone else is trying to take it. Your bank's real name shows on the screen — caller ID can be forged. The voice is calm, gives a case number, and knows your recent transactions. Then comes the instruction: for your protection, move your balance into a safe account we've opened for you. And you do it. You transfer it yourself, feeling grateful, because the moment you believe someone is defending you, you stop defending yourself. That's the entire design. Every warm feeling you have during that call is the product. This is now one of the largest categories of fraud loss in the UK.

**ENDING LINE:** "No real bank will ever ask you to move money to keep it safe. That sentence has no honest version."

## 10 — "Why gift cards" · *The payment tell*

**HOOK (0–2s):** "No government on Earth accepts gift cards. So why do scammers always ask for them?"

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:** WHY GIFT CARDS?

**VOICEOVER:**
> No tax office, no police force, no utility company on Earth accepts payment in gift cards. So why is it always gift cards? Because of what happens to the money. A bank transfer leaves a trail, has a name at the other end, and can sometimes be clawed back. A gift card is different. Once you read the numbers off the back to someone on the phone, the value is gone — instantly, anonymously, and from anywhere in the world. The physical card in your hand is already empty. That's why they never want the card posted. They only ever want the numbers, and they want you to stay on the line while you read them.
