## Notes

**Titles are truncated around 40-50 characters in the feed**, so both hooks
finish early: a viewer sees `Why one penny beats $1,000,000 —` and
`Why Germans used money as wallpaper —` before the cut. The search keyword
after the dash is there for the algorithm, not the swipe.

**Deliberately omitted tags.** The linter suggests every channel-level
hashtag on every video, so it asks for `compoundinterest` on the
hyperinflation Short and `inflation` on the penny Short. Both were left off.
Tags that don't describe the video are tag stuffing and work against you —
each tag above is actually about the video it sits on.

**Title is the biggest single lever on a Short.** If one underperforms, swap
in a backup before rewriting anything else; it costs nothing and the video
keeps its watch history.
