# Herbs Decoded — Your First 10 Shorts (ready to produce)

Ten complete, ready-to-shoot herbal explainer Shorts across all five pillars in
`tools/niche-herbal.json`. Each is built to win the first 2 seconds and loop or
provoke a comment at the end.

**How to read each script:**
- **HOOK (0–2s)** — exact words on screen + said aloud in frame 1. 80% of the battle.
- **VO** — full voiceover, timed to ~15–30s. Narrate it warm and curious — a friend showing you something surprising about a plant you already own.
- **ON-SCREEN** — big text (most people watch muted first).
- **VISUALS** — what to shoot or generate; cut every 1.5–3s. Macro shots of real herbs perform well and cost nothing.
- **END** — the loop line or question (drives re-watch + comments).
- **TITLE / DESC** — paste-ready. Run through `tools/optimize_metadata.py` before posting.

Narration target: ~2.5 words/second (a 20s Short ≈ 50 words). Keep it tight.

**The hard rule for this niche:** explain mechanisms and history, state the
evidence level honestly, and never claim an herb *cures, treats, or prevents*
anything. YouTube suppresses and can strike misleading health claims — and the
honest framing ("here's what the studies actually show") is itself the hook.
Keep a standing line in every description: *"Educational only — not medical
advice."*

## Packaging notes for this niche

- Macro herb footage is the thumbnail cheat code: one herb, huge in frame, on a dark background, 3–4 word overlay ("MINT IS LYING").
- The honesty angle is the differentiator — lean into "what studies actually say" in titles. The wellness space is saturated with overclaiming; being the channel that *doesn't* is the brand.
- Keep the disclaimer line in every description. Never use "cures," "treats," "heals," or "prevents" in any title, description, or on-screen text.
