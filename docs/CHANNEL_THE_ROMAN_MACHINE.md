# The Roman Machine

A standalone channel, split off from The Wealth Paradox. The money Shorts stay
there; Roman history lives here.

**Why split.** One channel posting both was teaching the algorithm two
audiences at once. A viewer arriving from a compound-interest video and a
viewer arriving from an arena video want different next-videos, and satisfying
neither is worse than serving one.

## What the channel is

> How the Roman Empire actually worked — the machinery of power, spectacle and
> punishment, and the people it ground through.

Not a facts-about-Rome channel. The two videos already made share one shape:
**Rome made a show of its power, and someone paid for it.** Every script ends
on a flat one-line reframe rather than a summary —

- *"Death was partly theatre."*
- *"Sworn to a Caesar who had forgotten them... and in the end, they paid the price."*

That closing beat is the format. Keep it.

## Pillars

1. **Spectacle and Control** — the arena, the triumph, punishment as public theatre
2. **The Frontier** — legions, garrisons, the edge of the known world
3. **Power and Punishment** — law, sentencing, slavery, who the state could destroy
4. **The Machinery** — engineering, grain, roads, money, the logistics of empire
5. **The People Underneath** — soldiers, slaves, the condemned, the poor

Pillars 4 and 5 are deliberately not about death. Two videos in, the channel
reads grimmer than it needs to; an engineering or daily-life video widens the
range without breaking the voice.

## Voice

Plain, unsentimental, specific. State what the sources say and let it be
disturbing on its own — no adjectives doing work the fact should do. Never
"shocking", never "you won't believe".

## Ground rules

**Say only what the sources support.** The Britannia script is atmosphere
rather than claim: it evokes the northern frontier without naming a battle,
legion or year, which is fine as long as nobody answers "which battle?" in the
comments as though there were one. Where a video *does* make a claim, it should
survive a hostile classicist.

**Executions and slavery are the subject, not the spectacle.** Documentary
framing, no gore, no dwelling. The restraint is also what makes the closing
lines land.

## Production

- Niche config: [`tools/niche-rome.json`](../tools/niche-rome.json) — pass
  `--niche-file niche-rome.json` to the tools
- Existing metadata: [`METADATA_ROME_BEASTS.md`](METADATA_ROME_BEASTS.md),
  [`METADATA_EDGE_OF_WORLD.md`](METADATA_EDGE_OF_WORLD.md)
- Captions: ShortsCaptioner, `hormozi` preset, Anton. Gold is the only
  highlight colour that belongs in a torchlit palette — the neon presets fight
  it badly and `boxed` is illegible at this size.
