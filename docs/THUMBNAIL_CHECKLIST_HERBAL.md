# Herbs Decoded — Thumbnail & First-Frame Checklist

For Shorts, the **first frame** *is* the thumbnail in most places — it's what
shows in the feed, on your channel grid, and in search. Treat frame 1 of every
Short as a thumbnail you have to earn the swipe with. (You can also set a custom
thumbnail that appears on the channel grid and search; do both.)

Run this checklist on every upload before it goes out.

---

## The 5-second test

Before posting, glance at your first frame for 5 seconds and ask:
**"Would a stranger scrolling at full speed stop for this?"**
If you hesitate, it's not done. The image must promise the payoff instantly.

---

## Must-haves (every thumbnail / first frame)

- [ ] **One herb, huge in frame.** A single sprig, clove, root, or flower in
  macro — not a spice-rack collage. If a viewer can't name the plant in half a
  second, redo it.
- [ ] **The most striking visual, not a title card.** Lead with the clove
  mid-crush, the steam curling off the mug, the roots in the glass — never a
  logo or "Episode 4" slate.
- [ ] **High contrast.** Fresh green (or golden turmeric, purple lavender)
  against a dark, near-black background is this niche's signature look — the
  herb should glow off the frame.
- [ ] **Reads at thumbnail size.** Shrink it to the size of a fingernail — is
  the herb still obvious? Detail disappears at feed size; shape and contrast
  survive.
- [ ] **Curiosity, not explanation.** The image should raise a question
  ("why is the garlic smashed?", "what's wrong with the mint?"), not answer it.
- [ ] **A moment of action beats a still.** Mid-crush, mid-pour, mid-slice,
  steam mid-curl — frozen motion signals something is *happening*.

---

## Text on the image (keep it minimal)

- [ ] **3–4 words max**, if any. The feed is small; a sentence is unreadable.
- [ ] **Big, bold, high-contrast font.** One font across all Shorts for brand
  recognition. White or pale text with a dark stroke/shadow reads on any
  background.
- [ ] **Top third or a clear corner.** Keep text clear of the bottom — the
  Shorts UI (title, channel, buttons) covers the lower portion and right edge.
- [ ] **The contradiction lands hard.** "MINT IS LYING", "CRUSH IT FIRST",
  "NATURAL ≠ SAFE", "FREE MINT FOREVER" — a flipped assumption beats a topic
  label ("Peppermint Facts") every time.
- [ ] **Never a health claim.** No "CURES", "HEALS", "FIGHTS", "DETOX" on any
  frame — it invites suppression and breaks the honesty brand. The
  contradiction hook is stronger anyway.
- [ ] **Don't repeat the title verbatim.** The title is already shown; the
  on-image text should add a second hook, not duplicate.

---

## Brand consistency (so your grid looks like a channel, not a pile)

- [ ] **Same font + same text style** on every Short.
- [ ] **A consistent color signature** — vivid botanical color on near-black.
  Your channel grid should read as a dark shelf of glowing plants,
  recognizable as *yours* at a glance.
- [ ] **Optional corner badge** for the series name (Kitchen Herbs / How It
  Works / The Honest Answer / Became Medicine / Never Buy It Again) so viewers
  learn your formats.
- [ ] **No clutter, no watermarks, no stock-art look.** Real macro footage is
  your advantage — a phone camera an inch from a mint leaf beats generic
  wellness stock every time. Avoid the mortar-and-pestle-on-white cliché.

---

## Niche-specific wins (herbal explainers)

- [ ] **The familiar thing made strange** — a supermarket herb shot like a
  scientific specimen, or one wrong detail (a thermometer in a mint mojito,
  a pill next to tree bark) makes the everyday stop-worthy.
- [ ] **Show the transformation** — split frames sell mechanism instantly:
  whole clove | crushed clove; sprig | jar of roots; bark | aspirin pill.
- [ ] **Steam, droplets, and backlight** — backlit steam off a mug or water
  droplets on leaves add motion and premium feel for free.
- [ ] **The compound as a character** — a simple glowing molecule overlaid on
  the herb ("meet menthol") signals *science*, not folklore, and separates
  you from every wellness account.
- [ ] **Avoid the wellness clichés** — no lotus-hands, no white-background
  supplement bottles, no generic apothecary flat-lays. Specific and a little
  scientific beats soft and spiritual for this brand.

---

## Quick reject list (redo the frame if any are true)

- ✗ Subject is small or off-center
- ✗ First frame is a title card, logo, or black intro
- ✗ Text is more than ~4 words or hard to read shrunk down
- ✗ Important text/subject sits in the bottom or far-right (UI covers it)
- ✗ Low contrast — herb blends into background
- ✗ The image answers the question instead of raising one
- ✗ Any cure/heal/detox claim appears anywhere in the frame
- ✗ It doesn't look like the rest of your channel

---

### Workflow

1. Pick your single most arresting frame as frame 1 (usually the action or
   "wrong detail" moment).
2. Add ≤4 words of high-contrast text in your channel font, top third.
3. Shrink to fingernail size — still obvious? If not, fix contrast/size.
4. Set a matching custom thumbnail (for grid + search).
5. Confirm the title (run `tools/optimize_metadata.py`) doesn't just repeat
   the on-image text — and carries no health claim.
