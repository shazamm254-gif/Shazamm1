# Traffic Playbook

Two engines. Run both from day one.

---

# ENGINE 1 — INTENT TRAFFIC
### Starts paying: days 1–10. Low volume, high conversion, no audience needed.

You're finding people who are **already looking for the solution** and being
the most useful answer they find. This is the fastest path to a first sale,
and the first sale matters far more psychologically than financially.

## Where

| Platform | Why | First move |
|---|---|---|
| **Reddit** | Highest-intent free traffic on the internet | Find 3–5 subs for your niche's problem. Sort by New. |
| **Quora** | Answers rank in Google for years — one good answer earns for ages | Find questions with high views and weak top answers |
| **YouTube comments** | Comments on big videos get thousands of reads | Top 10 videos for your problem keyword. Comment early on new uploads. |
| **Facebook groups** | Buyers cluster; less competition than Reddit | Join 3–5, read for 2 days before posting |
| **Niche forums** | Old-school, low competition, very warm | Search "[niche] forum" |

## The rule that keeps you alive on these platforms

> **Give the whole answer for free. Make the link optional.**

Every one of these platforms will ban an account that shows up and drops
links. They will tolerate — and reward — an account that's genuinely helpful
and occasionally links something.

Practically:
- **First 7 days on a platform: zero links.** Build history and karma.
- Then: roughly **1 link per 10 helpful posts.**
- Never link in a first comment on a brand new account.
- When you do link, **disclose it**: "full disclosure, I get a commission if
  you buy through that — here's the direct link if you'd rather:"
  Disclosing *raises* conversion on Reddit. It reads as confidence.
- Never link where the sub rules forbid it. Read the sidebar.
- **Prefer linking your own bridge page or free lead magnet over a raw
  affiliate link.** Raw hoplinks are auto-removed on many subs. A page you
  own isn't.

## The answer template

```
[Direct answer to their exact question — 2 to 4 sentences, no hedging.]

[The context that makes it useful. The specific detail most people get
wrong. This is the part that earns the upvote.]

[What you'd do in their position, concretely.]

[Only if genuinely relevant:] I wrote up the longer version here — [link].
Fair warning, there's an affiliate link on that page.
```

## Daily quota

**5 answers a day.** Twenty minutes. Compounds silently — a Quora answer you
wrote in March still sends clicks in December.

# ENGINE 2 — CONTENT TRAFFIC
### Starts paying: weeks 3–8. High volume, compounds forever, can't be taken away.

This is your Shorts engine. You already have it built.

## Platform priority for affiliate purposes

| Platform | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts** | **Best.** Description links allowed, searchable, long tail, feeds a real channel. Your repo is already built for it. |
| **TikTok** | Huge reach, but bio link only and aggressive about affiliate behavior. Great top-of-funnel, keep the money step off-platform. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Bio link only. Good for DM-based conversion. |
| **Pinterest** | Sleeper. Direct links allowed, users are in buying mode, pins earn for years. Underrated for health/home/finance niches. |
| **Facebook Reels/pages** | Older demographic, which for many ClickBank health offers is exactly the buyer. |

**Post the same Short to all of them.** Same file, same day. The marginal cost
is two minutes and one platform will randomly outperform the others.

## Where the link goes

- **YouTube:** bridge link in the description, first line, plus pinned comment.
- **TikTok/IG:** bio link only. Say "link in bio" out loud in the video —
  people genuinely don't look.
- **Pinterest:** link directly on the pin.
- **Never** put a raw hoplink in a caption. Platforms downrank outbound
  affiliate links, and some block the domain outright. Always your bridge page.

## Link-in-bio

Use a free tool (Linktree, Beacons, or just your GitHub Pages bridge page).
Give each platform its **own tracking ID** so you know which platform sells —
`link_builder.py` does this automatically per channel.

## What makes a Short produce clicks instead of just views

Views and clicks are different skills. A video can get 500k views and zero
clicks. The click comes from an **open loop**:

1. **Name the specific problem in the first 2 seconds.** Not the category —
   the symptom. "If your ears ring when the room goes quiet" beats "tinnitus tips."
2. **Deliver genuine value in the video.** Give them something that works.
   Trust is what gets clicked.
3. **Leave exactly one thing unresolved** — and say what it is. "There's a
   fourth one that made the biggest difference, but it needs about two minutes
   to explain properly. It's in the description."
4. **Give one reason to go now.** Curiosity decays in seconds.

The mistake is teasing everything and delivering nothing. That gets views,
angry comments, and no clicks. Over-deliver in the video; hold back exactly
one thing.

## What NOT to do

| Don't | Why |
|---|---|
| Buy traffic on day one | You'll lose money learning what a $50 CPM is. Get free traffic converting first. |
| Run paid ads to a raw hoplink | Google/Meta ban it, and most vendors forbid it. |
| Bid on the vendor's brand name | Fastest way to be banned by the vendor. |
| Spam Facebook groups | Account death, and it doesn't work anyway. |
| Use fake before/after images or invented testimonials | FTC issue and network ban. See `06-COMPLIANCE.md`. |
| Promise income or cures | Same. This is the one that ends accounts. |
| Post one Short and check stats for an hour | Volume is the variable you control. Post, then go make the next one. |

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | Job |
|---|---|
| **Sunday** | Batch-produce the week's Shorts (5–10). Run the weekly report: *"What should I kill?"* |
| **Mon–Sat** | Post 1–3 Shorts. 5 intent answers. One email if list ≥ 20. |
| **Any day something works** | Tell me. We make five more of that one. |

Finding a winner and making 5 more of it is the entire game. Everything else
is just staying in long enough for it to happen.
